Ironbook is a workout log with the look of a gym blackboard: chalk on iron, tape, gold for records.

The whole point of a training log is knowing what you did last time while you stand at the bar. Ironbook puts that number next to every set, runs your rest timer on the Lock Screen, and draws the line going up.

FREE, FOREVER
• Unlimited workouts, templates and history
• Every set shows last session's weight and reps; tick a set and it copies them if you left them blank
• Rest timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a buzz when it's time
• A new challenge every month, with a gold medal when you finish it
• Warm-up sets worked out for you: empty bar, 40, 60 and 80 percent
• 1RM calculator with every percentage worked out, and plate maths with the bar drawn per side, in kg or lb
• Notes on any lift, and last time's note shown when you do it again
• A weekly goal, a week streak, and one free streak shield every month
• A finish screen that calls out every PR
• Your top three records, the small week widget, Lock Screen widgets and a Control Center button
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Start my workout in Ironbook", "How is my week in Ironbook"
